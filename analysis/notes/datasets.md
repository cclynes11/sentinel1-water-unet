# Candidate datasets

Ordered by priority. **UrbanSARFloods is the primary dataset and Sen1Floods11 is the secondary.**
The others are fallbacks or extra OOD test sets. Citations are verified in
[`citation_verification.md`](citation_verification.md).

Input compatibility with the Wagner et al. 2024 U-Net in this repo matters for every row. The model expects
**Sentinel-1 IW GRD, VV+VH, σ⁰ in dB scaled to 8 bits, 10 m, WGS84** (see `src/s1water/preprocess.py`, `README.md`).

| **2 (secondary)** | **Sen1Floods11** (Bonafilia et al., CVPRW 2020) | Paper: [CVF open access](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPRW_2020/html/w11/Bonafilia_Sen1Floods11_A_Georeferenced_Dataset_to_Train_and_Test_Deep_Learning_CVPRW_2020_paper.html) · Code/data: [github.com/cloudtostreet/Sen1Floods11](https://github.com/cloudtostreet/Sen1Floods11) (GCS bucket per README) | 4,831 chips of 512×512; 120,406 km²; 11 flood events; 6 continents; 14 biomes; 357 ecoregions. ## Recommendation (bullets)
- Train / fine-tune and run the main OOD analysis on **UrbanSARFloods**, with a leave-events-out split.
- Apply the **Wagner U-Net zero-shot to Sen1Floods11** first: least preprocessing risk, gives an early result for the interim report.
- Keep **Kuro Siwo** in reserve for more events if the correlations are underpowered.
- Landscape covariates for proxies (land cover, DEM/HAND, permanent water) come from external layers. Pick and cite them in the methods. **They have not been verified here.**
